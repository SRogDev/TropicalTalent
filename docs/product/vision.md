# Vision — Cubora

> Status: working plan, not a frozen specification. Product behavior, commercial rules, UX, architecture, and implementation details keep evolving as Cubora is refined.

## What Cubora is

Cubora is a commercial platform connecting Cuba's human talent, services, products, producers, and production capabilities with buyers around the world.

Cubora is not simply a marketplace or directory. It is an **AI-powered commercial intermediary** that helps buyers discover opportunities, find reliable suppliers, negotiate, and establish commercial relationships.

**Core principle:** make Cuban capabilities accessible to the world through technology, trust, and commercial coordination.

## Brand

- **Name:** Cubora.
- **Tagline:** Born from ingenuity. Built through resilience. Made in Cuba.
- **Personality:** global, technological, commercially professional, and distinctly Cuban.
- **Visual direction:** contemporary and minimal, light green as the primary brand color.
- **Design philosophy:** international credibility without losing Cuban identity. Avoid tourist clichés, stereotypical imagery, and excessive visual decoration.

## Made in Cuba

A recognizable seal for products genuinely made, grown, or manufactured in Cuba.

- Origin is verified before the seal is awarded.
- Origin verification is separate from quality certification.
- The seal is a recognizable identity and authenticity indicator across the platform.

## What success looks like

The initial commercial priority is acquiring **real buyers**, not maximizing the number of listings. The metrics that matter (see `docs/marketing/go-to-market.md`):

- Qualified buyer conversations.
- Real sourcing requests.
- Verified supplier matches.
- Proposals delivered.
- Accepted commercial opportunities.
- Completed operations.
- Intermediation revenue.
- Repeat buyers.

## Current state (2026-11-12)

Phases 0–5 of the MVP plan are implemented in this repository as a **reference build**: brand and foundation, unified catalog, AI discovery on LangGraph, commercial operations (CRM, mediated negotiation, contact release, fee records), and quality/launch hardening. See `STATUS.md` for the per-phase verification results and the honest not-working list.

The MVP is **not launched**. Launch is blocked on Roger's open decisions — the fee model, the contact-release policy, the payment/compliance route, commercial terms, and the launch bar — recorded in `docs/product/commercial-model.md`. No payments, no custody of third-party funds, and no live deployment exist in this build, by design.
